# ORVIA — Research Integrity Statement

> **Every important claim in ORVIA must be traceable back to user-linked evidence and original sources.**

---

## What "detected" and "observed" mean in ORVIA

ORVIA uses the following language precisely:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Detected** | A pattern was found in the database by a deterministic algorithm (e.g., a concept appears in 5+ sources) |
| **Observed** | A statistical count was computed from the actual records (e.g., this concept appeared 12 times) |
| **Supported** | A finding has at least one explicitly linked evidence record |
| **Needs evidence** | A finding exists but has no linked evidence records |
| **Emerging** | A concept's appearance rate increased over the most recent time window relative to earlier windows |

ORVIA never claims that a pattern proves a conclusion. It shows you what is measurable in your own research collection so you can draw your own conclusions.

---

## What ORVIA Never Does

- **Never generates research conclusions.** ORVIA surfaces patterns; the researcher interprets them.
- **Never invents sources.** Every source in ORVIA was explicitly imported by the user.
- **Never invents evidence.** Every evidence record was explicitly created by the user.
- **Never invents citations.** Every citation in a report links to a real source that was imported and a real evidence record that was created.
- **Never fabricates statistics.** Every count, frequency, or score is computed directly from database records.
- **Never uses LLMs or generative AI.** There is no text generation in ORVIA. All content is user-created or algorithmically computed from user data.
- **Never contacts external services.** ORVIA is fully offline. No API calls, no telemetry, no analytics pings.

---

## Report Provenance

A research report in ORVIA is not a generated summary. It is a structured assembly of:

1. **The investigation's research question** — written by you.
2. **The sources you attached** — imported and selected by you.
3. **The evidence you recorded** — extracted and annotated by you.
4. **The findings you wrote** — authored by you, with explicit evidence links.
5. **Deterministic statistics** — counts and patterns computed from your records.

The synthesis engine arranges these into a structured document with traceable citations. It does not add, invent, or infer any content beyond what you explicitly recorded.

---

## Quality Indicators

ORVIA's report quality analyzer produces indicators that reflect structural completeness:

| Indicator | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Evidence coverage** | Percentage of findings that have at least one linked evidence record |
| **Source diversity** | Number of distinct sources cited in the report |
| **Concept coverage** | Number of key investigation concepts addressed |
| **Open question ratio** | Fraction of research questions that remain unresolved |

These indicators do not measure the accuracy, truth, or importance of your research. They measure structural completeness of the research record.

---

## The Researcher's Responsibility

ORVIA is a tool that helps you organize, explore, and structure your research. The researcher remains fully responsible for:

- Evaluating the credibility of imported sources
- Assessing the validity of evidence
- Drawing appropriate conclusions from patterns
- Ensuring that findings are accurate
- Verifying that citations are correct

ORVIA does not validate external facts. It is not a fact-checker. It is not an oracle. It is a structured workspace that makes your research process more organized and traceable.
